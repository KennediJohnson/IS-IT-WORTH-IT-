# Worth the Hype?

A colorful little app that helps you decide whether a purchase is actually worth your money.

- **Regret Test**: answer a few quick questions and get a score out of 100, with every point explained
- **Cost per use**: log each time you use something and watch the price per use drop
- **Spending**: see your last six months against your budget, and how much you've saved by skipping
- **Community**: boards, comments, photos, plus private iMessage-style chats with emoji, GIFs and read receipts

Install it on your phone: open the site, then tap **Share → Add to Home Screen** (iPhone) or **Install app** (Android).
It works offline, and your data stays on your phone. No AI and no outside services are used.
